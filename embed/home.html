Welcome! I'm Nicholas Bramhall, a Computer Engineering student at Georgia Institute of Technology with a passion for hardware and digital design. From researching AI code comparison in high school to diving into digital signal processing at GT, I'm driven by the challenge of building systems from the ground up, at the hardware level where software meets silicon.
