With this in mind, how will this end goal be accomplished? As mentioned above my course load is heavily curated to that sector of computer engineering. Of course, right after that jump straight into industry, hopefully close to where I want to be but if not, just getting my foot in the door and working my way over to the CPU and GPU sector.
